AI SECURITY · SYSTEMS

Ziyu Liu.

Ph.D. student / Binghamton University ↗

I study how to protect neural networks
and deploy them securely.

My research focuses on LLM security and model protection. I explore trusted execution environments for protecting model weights and investigate model extraction risks to inform more effective defenses.

My earlier work spans secure on-device DNN inference and multi-tenant FPGA security. Across these topics, I am interested in the trade-offs between security, model utility, and system performance.
